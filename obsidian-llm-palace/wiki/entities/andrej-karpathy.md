# Andrej Karpathy

## About

Co-founder of OpenAI, former Director of AI at Tesla. One of the clearest explainers of machine learning concepts working today. Founded Eureka Labs (July 2024) focused on AI education.

## Notable Work

- Published the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] gist (April 2026) — a blueprint for LLM-maintained knowledge bases
- LLM101n course through Eureka Labs
- Neural network training lectures (widely viewed on YouTube)
- Led Tesla's Autopilot computer vision team
